First FALAN Latin American Training Program in Neuroscience

A three-week, hands-on neuroscience school for early-career researchers from across Latin America — lectures, laboratory workshops, and mentorship with leading neuroscientists, hosted across Santiago and Valparaíso, Chile.

Overview

Three weeks, two cities, one cohort

Around 27–28 students selected from across Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, México, Peru, and Uruguay spend three weeks working directly with leading Latin American neuroscientists — lectures and hands-on laboratory workshops hosted at USACH and partner institutions.

Week 1

Santiago

Lectures and hands-on workshops at USACH and partner universities.

Week 2

Valparaíso

A full week of workshops on the coast.

Week 3

Santiago

The program concludes back in Santiago.
